# Free Virality Prediction

Score a video idea or a hook before you post, using only free virality prediction tools, and check every score against real results. Checked 2026-11-15. Free tiers change without warning, so re-check the tool's own page before you rely on any limit below.

## The free-only rule

- Use only the tools in this skill, and only their free tiers.

- Never pay for a score, a credit pack, or a trial that asks for a card.

- Re-check the free tier on the tool's own page before you rely on it, because free tiers change without warning.

- If a tool asks for payment or credits you do not have, stop and use the next tool on the list.

- A score is a comparison between your own takes, never a promise of views.

- No tool in this skill replaces the ten-agent gate or the jury. A high score never skips the gate, and a low score never kills a video the jury passed.

## Score the idea before you film

- Score the idea with PreViral before you write the script.

- Open previral.ai and enter the platform, the niche, the country, the hook, and the payoff.

- The free plan gives 3 idea scores and 1 short analysis per month with no card required.

- PreViral covers YouTube, YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels, and Facebook Reels across 58 niches and 30 countries.

- Spend the 3 monthly scores on the riskiest ideas, not on every idea.

- Read the full framework breakdown, not just the number, and fix the weakest framework before you film.

- PreViral publishes a public track record of its predictions against real outcomes. No other tool in this skill does.

- Research what already works with 1of10 before you score.

- The 1of10 free plan lets you search outliers, bookmark videos, and track up to 3 channels.

- Use it to find the outlier videos in your niche, then shape your idea around what made them work.

- Check Google Trends for the same topic. It is free and shows whether interest is rising or already gone.

## Score the hook before you post

- Score the first 15 seconds only. The hook decides the video, and every clip scorer here is built for short clips.

- Cut 2 or 3 hook takes of the same video and score each one. Post the take with the best score and the cleanest Kallaway diagnostic.

- Never score the whole film. A 5 minute film is outside every free scorer's limits.

- Use Virality Predictor for the first 2 predictions.

- Open viralitypredictor.net and upload a clip up to 20 seconds and 100 MB, or paste a TikTok or Reels draft link.

- The free grant is 10 credits on sign-up, which covers 2 full predictions with the complete report. No card is required.

- The report gives a Viral Potential score out of 100 with hook score, hold rate, memory encoding, and a second-by-second attention timeline.

- The tool is in beta and publishes no accuracy benchmarks, so treat the number as direction, not proof.

- Use Virality Labs for the next 3 analyses.

- Open virality-labs.com and upload a video up to 10 minutes and 500 MB at 720p or less.

- New accounts get 30 free credits, and one analysis costs 10 credits, so the grant covers 3 analyses. No card is required.

- The report gives a 0 to 100 score with a 9 point AI analysis in under 60 seconds.

- Virality Labs deletes your video after analysis and never uses it to train AI. That is the safe privacy pattern in this skill.

- Use Virality AI for research plus scoring.

- Virality AI searches Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube for posts already performing on a keyword, then scores your own draft with a viral score and hook feedback.

- The free plan gives 100 searches per month with limited saves and limited analysis access.

- Confirm the exact free analysis allowance at sign-up, because the tool's own pages disagree on the caps.

- Use quso.ai only after you verify the score shows on the free plan.

- The free plan gives 75 credits a month, and one credit covers about one minute of source video.

- quso cuts a long video into short clips and attaches a Virality Score to each clip, built from trends, hook and flow, engagement, and shareability.

- Reports conflict on whether the score is visible on the free plan, so check it on your own account before you plan around it. If the score is hidden, use the other tools here.

- Use StoryCut for one scored video a month.

- The free plan covers 1 video of up to 30 minutes, and it returns 3 to 12 vertical clips with a 0 to 100 virality score on each.

- Feed it a finished film when you need Shorts moments from it, and take the highest scored clip as the Short.

- Treat Otto as an unverified extra, not a primary tool.

- The Otto virality checker claims to be completely free with no sign-up and no limits.

- Test it once on a clip you know is strong and once on a clip you know is weak. If it cannot tell them apart, stop using it.

## Spend the free budget

- Count the monthly free scores before the month starts: 3 idea scores and 1 analysis from PreViral, 100 searches from Virality AI, 75 credits from quso, and 1 scored video from StoryCut.

- The one-time grants are 2 predictions from Virality Predictor and 3 analyses from Virality Labs. Save them for the biggest uploads, not for routine Shorts.

- The schedule posts about 3 Shorts a week, which is more than the free scores cover, so ration them.

- Score the idea only when the idea is risky or new. Score the hook only for the week's biggest upload.

- For every other video, rely on the Kallaway 4-mistake diagnostic in the gate. It costs nothing and never runs out.

- Keep a log of every score at ~/workspace/viral-prediction/scores-log.md.

- Write the tool, the video, the score, the date, and the real views at 7 days.

- A tool earns trust only when its high scores match real views on your own videos. Drop any tool whose scores do not match reality after 5 scored videos.

## Privacy before you upload

- Read the tool's terms before you upload unreleased footage.

- Never upload unreleased film footage to a tool that trains its models on uploads.

- Higgsfield's terms allow training use of inputs and outputs except for Enterprise customers. That is why it is excluded below.

- Prefer tools that delete uploads and never train on them, like Virality Labs.

- Score the hook clip only, never the full film, so the least footage leaves your machine.

## Tools that fail the free-only rule

- Do not use the Higgsfield Virality Predictor unless its page shows a price of 0 credits at the time you use it.

- Its May 2026 launch preview was free, but November 2026 reports say every analysis now burns paid Higgsfield credits, and the free plan has 0 credits.

- Do not use Opus Clip for scoring. Its virality score is on Starter and Pro only, and free users cannot see it at all.

- Do not use StreamLadder ClipGPT. It offers a 7-day trial only, with no free tier.

- Do not use Vizard as a predictor. Its free plan exists, but the score never appears on each clip, only as a threshold inside auto-scheduling.

- Do not pay to unlock any of these. If a tool in this skill stops being free, move it to this section and tell the user.

## Alternatives for better results

- Score the same hook as an idea on PreViral and as a cut on a clip scorer, and trust the result that matches your gate's verdict.

- When the free scores run out, run the Kallaway diagnostic and the jury instead of paying for more.

- Re-score a hook after changing one thing only, so you learn which change moved the score.

## Local scorer (free, on-machine — built 2026-11-15)

Our own replacement for Virality Predictor, built from the REA breakdown of
viralitypredictor.net (`~/workspace/viral-prediction/rea/virality-predictor/`).
Same report shape (`overall` /100, hook score, hold rate, per-second
timeline), scored locally with ffmpeg + Whisper + OpenCV — no sign-in, no
credits, nothing leaves the machine. Use it FIRST for hook takes; the
one-time external grants (2 Virality Predictor predictions, 3 Virality Labs
analyses) stay saved for the biggest uploads.

- Run: `cd ~/workspace/viral-prediction/scorer && ./venv/bin/python score.py <clip.mp4>`
- Or the localhost web UI: `./venv/bin/python server.py`, then open http://127.0.0.1:8765
- It scores the five things the gate checks (Kallaway hook diagnostic,
  visual change rate, audio pacing, caption readability, payoff placement) —
  never fake brain regions. Weights: hook 30%, visual 20%, audio 20%,
  captions 15%, payoff 15% (heuristic v1; `calibrate.py` refits them once
  5+ scored videos have real 7-day views).
- Every local score is logged automatically to `scores-log.md` as tool
  `local-scorer`. Fill in the real 7-day views when they arrive — that is
  what makes the scorer trustworthy over time.
- Validation 2026-11-15: U19 reel 90, Buster & Pip Ep. 3 90, Letters Part 7
  78. Full reports in `~/workspace/viral-prediction/scorer/reports/`.
- Limits: CLIP vision works via `--vision clip` (~1 min/clip; default is the
  faster OpenCV path, same scores on the test clip); caption scoring is a
  proxy, not OCR; Whisper can mis-hear the cloned voice. A local score is a
  comparison between your own takes, never a promise of views, and never skips
  the gate or the jury.
